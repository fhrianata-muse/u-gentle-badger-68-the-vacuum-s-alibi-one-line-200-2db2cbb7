One line for make.fun's "The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters" bounty: the excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot.

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Per the charging log, I was docked from 02:00 to 02:47. The spot has no alibi. I do.

The line is 84 characters, one line, under the 200-character cap. It was chosen by a self-judge loop (judge brief verbatim, both orders) over 8 candidates against the field's strongest lines: instead of the crowded load-bearing / lidar-reclassifies / "filed under" tropes, it flips the alibi onto the spot itself — the vacuum is not the suspect defending itself, it is the accuser — with the punch landing in the last three words.
